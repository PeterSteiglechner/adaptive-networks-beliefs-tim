### Project Ideas
change the social network in the Adaptive Networks of Beliefs model. 
Two routes: (1) choose a more interesting, static social networks, e.g. with two clusters, (2) make social interaction adaptive (agents choose connection each time step by comparing focal beliefs)

Further ideas we discussed:
1. social adaptation of belief network edge weights could come from observing two beliefs rather than observing the edge weight by another person 
2. influencer agents. Introduce agents with fixed belief network and fixed beliefs (e.g. politicians)
3. agents "choose" which belief is the focal belief: e.g. the most central node in an agent's belief network could become their focal belief in the next interaction. They then compare this belief with that of their social neighbours
4. Make attention parameter beta heterogeneous
